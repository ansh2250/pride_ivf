Frequently Asked Questions
What fertility treatments do you offer?

We offer a wide range of fertility treatments, including IVF, ICSI, IUI, egg freezing, and more. Our specialists work closely with patients to design personalized treatment plans.

How do I schedule a consultation?

You can schedule a consultation by calling our clinic directly or filling out the online contact form on our website. Our team will get in touch with you to confirm your appointment.

What is the success rate of IVF?

Our success rates vary depending on the specific treatment and patient circumstances. However, we pride ourselves on consistently achieving high success rates for IVF procedures.

Are treatments covered by insurance?

Insurance coverage depends on the specific plan. We recommend contacting your insurance provider for details, and our team can assist you with any required documentation.

How long does an IVF cycle take?

The typical IVF cycle takes about 4-6 weeks. This includes the stimulation of the ovaries, egg retrieval, fertilization, embryo culture, and embryo transfer.

What are the risks of IVF treatment?

While IVF is generally safe, there are risks such as multiple pregnancies, ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS), and emotional stress. Our doctors will thoroughly discuss these risks with you beforehand.
